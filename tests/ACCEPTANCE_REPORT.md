### 1. Product demo (`example-saas-demo`): 1920×1080, 23.5 s
- **Exercised:** title, browser device frame with URL bar, focus ring on a real UI element plus a label chip, UI punch-in zoom, contained logo card, bullets, end card with wipe, manual captions, synthesized music and SFX, SRT/VTT export.
- **Checks:** resolution, fps, duration (23.5 s exact), yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz, −14.0 LUFS, no clipping, the end decays, and the subtitles are valid.
- **Warnings:** the black segment at 15.5–18.3 s is the bullets scene (text on near-black), and the frozen segment at 0.7–2.8 s is the held title card. Both are intended.
- **Defects found in visual review, then fixed and re-verified:**
  - Captions overlapped the bottom of the zoomed screen; the device box is now 72% height when shown alone.
  - The square logo was letterboxed on black; contained images now render as a card.

### 2. Vlog (`example-travel-vlog`): 1080×1920, 16.6 s
- **Exercised:**
  - Recomposition from 16:9 to 9:16 (crop-to-fill).
  - Mixed containers (MP4/MOV) and sample rates (48 kHz and 44.1 kHz audio).
  - A crossfade, a hard cut and a fade through black.
  - An image clip with zoom, a warm grade, and center, lower and chapter titles.
  - Natural ambience plus a synthesized music bed, and fades in and out.
- **Checks:** duration matches the computed cut list (4 clips minus 2 transitions = 16.6 s); −13.9 LUFS; no black or frozen segments.
- **Defect found, then fixed:** xfade failed after a concat because of mismatched timebases. Every clip and join is now normalised to 1/fps.

### 3. Explainer (`example-ai-agents-explainer`): 1920×1080, 25.97 s
- **Exercised:**
  - 5 narration lines via TTS, with scene durations driven by narration cues (`untilCue`).
  - The light "paper" theme.
  - An animated diagram with a travelling loop pulse, and bullets with past items dimmed.
  - Karaoke-style captions, SRT/VTT (5 cues, wrapped at 2×42 characters), and no music.
- **Warnings:** frozen segments at 2.9–6.2 s and 18.1–21.4 s are static title and bullet cards, which suit a calm explainer.
- **Defects found in visual review, then fixed and re-verified:**
  - Highlighted caption words were low-contrast on the light theme.
  - Letter tips peeked through the mask reveal.
  - A diagram edge label sat on top of its line.

### Additional checks
- `--draft` renders at half resolution, and validation expects half-size output: verified on the explainer (960×540, same duration).
- A `blank` template project renders with `audio.none`, and validation correctly expects no audio stream.
- Intake is idempotent: re-scanning unchanged files takes 0.1 s and reports "0 new, 0 changed". The library intake works on an empty `assets/`.
- `doctor` reports the Whisper module UNAVAILABLE (no model), and every other module still works.

## Not verified

- **Audio was measured, not heard:** loudness, peaks, silence and decay. Nobody listened to the mixes.
- **Transcription:** `transcribe-whisper` is untested end to end because no model is installed; only its availability check was exercised.
- **Untested templates:** `product-launch`, `talking-head`, `short-form` and `long-form` have no dedicated test. Their scene types are covered by the tests above. The vertical layout of the Remotion engine (`device`/`title` in portrait) was not rendered; vertical was tested only on the FFmpeg path.
- **Not exercised:** user-supplied SRT/VTT as caption input (`captions: {file}`) and custom fonts via `timeline.fonts`.
- **`analyze`:** run on a talking-head clip and a vlog clip. The adaptive pause detection reproduced a hand-made pause map. Scene detection ran and correctly found no cuts in either single-take source; a source that actually contains cuts was not tested.
- **macOS/Linux:** never tested. TTS is Windows-only by design, and the system font fallbacks include common Linux/macOS paths.
