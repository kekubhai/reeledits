# Template: product-launch

Launch film: hook, reveal, features in device frames, CTA. Remotion engine.

## Creative guidance

Hook within 3 s; reveal on a musical accent; one feature per scene (device scene with highlights on REAL UI); restrained yellow-wipe style transitions only at big moments; end card with verified URL/store only. Never invent claims or numbers.

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
