# Template: vlog

Cinematic vlog/montage: natural audio, crossfades, warm grade, minimal titles. FFmpeg only.

## Creative guidance

Let moments breathe; natural sound is part of the story. Minimal titles (place/date). Crossfades sparingly, mostly hard cuts on action. No product-ad graphics.

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
