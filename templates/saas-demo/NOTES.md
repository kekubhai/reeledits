# Template: saas-demo

Screen-recording demo: browser/phone frames, UI zooms, focus rings, captions.

## Creative guidance

UI is the hero: keep it readable (fit, not crop), zoom into the region being used ({zoom:{at,scale,x,y}}), focus rings on real elements. Cut dead time; do not speed through important interactions. Grade "none" for screen recordings.

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
