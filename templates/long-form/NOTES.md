# Template: long-form

YouTube long-form: chapters, steady pacing, lower thirds, captions. Remotion or FFmpeg.

## Creative guidance

Use chapter title cards; keep graphics consistent across a long runtime; render drafts per section (scenes with `at`) when iterating. Consider ffmpeg-edit for long talking segments (faster).

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
