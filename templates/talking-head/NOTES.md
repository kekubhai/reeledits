# Template: talking-head

Speaker-led video: graded footage, captions, lower third, light B-roll.

## Creative guidance

Preserve natural faces (no heavy smoothing); grade "lift-face" helps back-lit selfie footage. Remove dead pauses using `analyze` (silence-detect), never mid-word. Captions improve retention. Do not cover the face with graphics.

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
