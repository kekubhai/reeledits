# Template: explainer

Educational explainer: narration, diagrams, bullets, subtitles. Calm, readable.

## Creative guidance

Clarity over spectacle: no cinematic wipes, little or no music, high-contrast light theme, one idea per scene. Scenes can end on narration cues (untilCue). Use diagram scenes for processes; bullets for lists. Prefer a recorded human voice when available (audio.tracks role "voice").

This is a starting point. Change scene types, theme, method or format whenever the brief calls for it.
